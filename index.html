About

Dr. Bharat Pathivada is a Research Scientist in the Department of Civil and Architectural Engineering and Mechanics at the University of Arizona and Center Manager of the Center for Applied Transportation Sciences (CATS). With over a decade of research experience in transportation engineering, his work focuses on advancing traffic safety and operations through data-driven methods. His research integrates statistical modeling, machine learning, traffic simulation, and intelligent transportation systems (ITS) to address complex transportation challenges. He works closely with state and local transportation agencies to translate research into practical solutions that improve traffic operations and roadway safety.
